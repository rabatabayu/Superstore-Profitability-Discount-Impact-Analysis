# 📊 Superstore Profitability & Discount Impact Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the **Superstore dataset** to understand how discount strategies, product categories, customer segments, regions, and time trends affect **sales and profitability**.

The main objective is to identify the factors contributing to profit and losses, particularly the impact of **high discount levels**, and provide data-driven recommendations to improve profitability.

Dataset Link:
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/vivek468/superstore-dataset-final/data

9994 Rows & 21 Column

---

## 🎯 Business Questions

How can the company maintain sales growth while improving profitability through more effective management of discounts, products, and regions?

## 🚀 Conclusion

The analysis shows that **higher sales do not always result in higher profitability**.

Discount strategy is one of the key factors affecting Superstore's profit performance. Discounts of **30% or more** are associated with negative aggregate margins, while certain product sub-categories such as **Tables and Bookcases** generate overall losses.

By controlling discount levels, improving the profitability of underperforming products, and focusing on profitable customer and regional segments, the business can pursue more sustainable growth.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

1. **Control high discount levels**  
   Discounts above 20% should be evaluated carefully because higher discount levels are strongly associated with negative profitability.

2. **Review loss-making sub-categories**  
   Investigate Tables and Bookcases to determine whether losses are caused by excessive discounts, pricing, product costs, or other operational factors.

3. **Prioritize profitable categories**  
   Maintain and strengthen Technology and Office Supplies while improving Furniture's profitability.

4. **Investigate regional differences**  
   Study the strategies and product mix used in the West region and evaluate whether similar approaches can be applied to weaker regions such as Central.

5. **Balance sales growth and profitability**  
   Business decisions should not focus only on increasing sales. Profit margin should also be monitored to ensure sustainable growth.
